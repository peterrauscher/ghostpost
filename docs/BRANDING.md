### GhostPost Branding Guidelines

#### Audience & Tone
- Audience: younger Gen Z preparing for college, jobs, or rush.
- Tone: friendly, reassuring, non-judgmental; light-hearted.

#### Typography
- Primary body: Inter preferred, platform system sans as a fallback.
- Accent/utility: `Space Mono` (numbers, small badges, code-like UI, microcopy emphasis). Avoid long paragraphs in monospace.

#### Color Palette
- Primary – Tekhelet: `#3D348B` (brand primary, headlines, primary CTAs)
- Secondary – Medium Slate Blue: `#7678ED` (secondary CTAs, highlights, gradients)
- Success – Fern Green: `#4C7240` (success, “All clear!” states)
- Text – Jet: `#303030` (primary text)
- Background – Baby Powder: `#FBF9F4` (app background)
- Warn – Selective Yellow: `#F7B801` (warnings, attention)
- Danger – Persimmon: `#F35B04` (destructive actions)
- Critical – Scarlet: `#F5381B` (critical errors, high risk)

#### Usage
- Backgrounds: Baby Powder as default; dark surfaces use Tekhelet overlays at 3–8%.
- Text: Jet for body; Tekhelet for headings/links.
- CTAs: Primary button uses Tekhelet background, Baby Powder text; hover/press darken by 6–10%.

#### Iconography & Logos
- Use provided SVGs in `assets/icons/` (Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook).

#### Motion
- Splash/ghost: gentle bob 2s loop; button press scale 0.95 with light haptics.
- Screen transitions: 300ms slide; progress ease-in-out.
- Swipes: springy but quick to settle (<300ms).

#### Do/Don’t
- Do: use Tekhelet/Slate Blue for brand presence; keep ghost mascot playful but not childish.
- Don’t: overuse Space Mono; avoid red tones for non-destructive elements; don’t recolor external platform logos.
