### GhostPost – Business Context

#### Product
- Mobile app to help Gen Z users audit and clean their social media history ahead of college/job applications or rush.

#### Core Jobs-To-Be-Done
- Quickly connect major platforms (Instagram, TikTok, X/Twitter, Facebook).
- Guide users to request/download platform data exports.
- Parse exports and flag risky content by category (substances, profanity, controversial topics, sexual content, negative behavior).
- Provide a fast swipe-review UI with keep/delete/unsure and a final deletion checklist.

#### MVP Scope
- Onboarding for concerns and platforms.
- Dashboard summarizing connection and review progress.
- Review UI scaffold; parsers and risk detection to follow.
- Results screen with export/sharing (future).

#### Positioning & Messaging
- Tagline: "Your past doesn't have to haunt you."
- Tone: helpful, non-judgmental, private.

#### User Flow
- User signs up with email/password or OAuth (X/Twitter, Facebook, Instagram).
- User connects the accounts they have on major platforms (Instagram, TikTok, X/Twitter, Facebook).
- User is guided through a quick review of their content.
- User can request/download platform data exports.
- User can review their content and delete/keep/unsure.

#### Future Monetization (exploratory)
- One-time unlock for batch features.
- Subscription for premium scanning and ongoing monitoring.
- Institutional partnerships (schools, fraternities/sororities).
